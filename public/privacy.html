Privacy / Updated Oct 2026

Your info

What the RSVP form collects

Your first and last name, stage name, Instagram handle, genre, location, phone, email, YouTube and profile links, website if you add one, and how you heard about URCypher. If you arrived from an ad or a tagged link, we also save the tag on that link so we know which post or ad sent you.

How it's used

The URCypher team uses it to listen to your music, decide on the lineup, and contact you about the cypher. Your RSVP is stored in a private Google Sheet the team manages. We also add your name and email to our Mailchimp list for URCypher updates, and every email has an unsubscribe link. We don't sell your information.

Meta Pixel

This site uses the Meta Pixel to measure how many people visit from our Instagram and Facebook posts and ads, and how many of them RSVP. The pixel uses cookies and tells Meta that a page was viewed or an RSVP was sent. It does not send your name, email, phone number or any form answers. You can control how Meta uses this activity in your Meta ad preferences, and you can block or clear cookies in your browser.

Questions or removal

To see, change or remove what you sent us, email URCypherTeam@gmail.com.
